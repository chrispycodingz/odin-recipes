Chicken Parm Recipe

Description

Chicken parm. Arguably another one of the best dishes ever invented. This Italian dish is comforting, satiating and indulgent.

Ingredients

  • 4 boneless, skinless chicken breasts, halved horizontally into 8 cutlets
  • 1/3 cup all-purpose flour
  • 2 large eggs, beaten
  • 1 cup panko breadcrumbs and 1/2 cup Italian-style breadcrumbs (panko adds great crunch)
  • 1/2 cup freshly grated Parmesan cheese
  • 1/2 tsp salt and 1/2 tsp black pepper (for the breadcrumbs)
  • 2 cups high-quality marinara sauce (or your favorite tomato passata)
  • 2 cups shredded or sliced low-moisture mozzarella cheese
  • 1/3 cup freshly grated Parmesan cheese
  • 1/2 cup olive oil (for frying the cutlets)
  • Fresh basil or parsley, roughly chopped (for garnish)

Steps

  1. Pound chicken: Slice 2 boneless chicken breasts in half horizontally and pound with a mallet to a 1/2-inch thickness.
  2. Set up station: Arrange three shallow bowls with 1/2 cup flour, 2 beaten eggs, and 1 cup panko breadcrumbs mixed with 1/2 cup grated Parmesan.
  3. Dredge: Coat chicken in flour, dip in egg, then press into breadcrumbs. Let rest for 10 minutes.
  4. Pan-fry: Heat 1/2 inch of olive oil in a skillet over medium-high heat. Cook chicken for 2 minutes per side until golden.
  5. Assemble: Transfer chicken to a baking dish. Top each piece with 2 tablespoons tomato sauce, mozzarella, and provolone cheese.
  6. Bake: Bake at 450°F (230°C) for 15 to 20 minutes until the cheese bubbles and the internal chicken temperature reaches 165°F (74°C).
